  • Content that offers Matching Mode shares entry count and gate progress across Normal/Hard/Nightmare difficulties and Matching Mode.
  • If you enter Matching Mode solo or as a party/raid and leave the dungeon during the run, your entry count is deducted even if you didn’t obtain gate rewards.
  • If content stops because a Matching Mode abort vote passes before rewards are obtained, entry count is restored. Characters present in the dungeon when content stopped have entry count restored; characters who left the dungeon at that point do not.

  • On death during Matching Mode, you can revive an unlimited number of times. On death, consume a Feather of Revival to revive instantly. ※ Without a Feather of Revival, you can revive by consuming 10 Crystals.
  • If you don’t revive within the revive wait time after death, you are ejected from the dungeon and entry count is consumed. ※ If ejected for not reviving in time, an entry-count restore item is not provided even if the remaining members vote to stop. ※ However, if you entered Matching Mode solo and were ejected mid-run, an entry-count restore item usable until the next regular maintenance is provided.

  • In Matching Mode, if you don’t take part in combat for a set time after it begins, you are warned. ※ Continuing not to participate after the warning ejects you and consumes entry count. ※ Playing a cutscene or waiting to revive after death does not count as non-participation. ※ If ejected for non-participation, an entry-count restore item is not provided even if remaining members vote to stop.

  • In Matching Mode, if the party has no support-type class, a combat-support buff is applied. ※ When a support-type class runs Matching Mode alone, an even stronger “damage dealt to enemies increased” effect is applied. ※ Bard, Paladin, Artist, and Valkyrie are classified by role based on their currently set “Enlightenment” Tier-1 passive.

Green = buff · Red = nerf. Color coding is applied to the changed values below and is best-effort (higher damage/uptime is a buff; higher cooldown, mana, or resource cost is a nerf).

Developer Comment

This class balance adjustment focuses on the play patterns that have shifted since the last pass, and on easing the operational burden of certain classes and setups observed in the current combat environment.

In particular, centering on structures where skill linking and combat performance did not flow naturally, we adjusted class mechanics, supplemented specific setups, and tuned PVE damage and the values of some Ark Passives and Ark Grids.

Soulfist’s overall combat structure was reworked so that each setup’s combat direction and resource management are clearly expressed. We clarified the classification of Martial (Muyy-gong) and Energy (Ki-gong) skills to distinguish combat direction by Ark Passive, and established a new linked combat flow via the new resource “True Energy” (Jin-gi) and the identity skill “Formless Art” (Musang Singong).

Also, Inner Energy was changed to a structure acquired actively through play; Hype was tuned to use its duration strategically; and Flash Step’s chain handling and cooldown structure were improved so mobility and combat flow feel smoother.

“Burst” Deathblade and “Sound of the Moon” Reaper are setups designed to deliver combat performance commensurate with their high skill requirement. This adjustment keeps the existing combat concept but eases the excessively risen operational burden, so each setup’s unique play feel carries through stably. In particular, “Burst” Deathblade was improved around the Burst Enhance effect and Blade Arts handling, and “Sound of the Moon” Reaper around the Persona state and ambush-skill linking.

“Overflowing Communion” Summoner had the burden of constantly managing summons and cases where movement restrictions during some skills broke combat flow. We raised the stability of key summons and eased some skills’ operational burden so the Communion setup’s signature sustained combat unfolds naturally.

Alongside this, we adjusted PVE damage considering performance changes and setup usage in key content, and corrected the values of some Ark Passives and Ark Grids based on per-level efficiency and usage so each effect fulfills its intended role. We’ll keep monitoring per-class performance and setup usage and continue refining so each class delivers a stable combat contribution and play experience in practice.
